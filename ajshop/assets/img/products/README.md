# Product photos — drop your images here

To use your **own real product photos**, add an image named after the product ID
below (JPG, ideally square, ~800×800px). It is picked up automatically and takes
priority over the fallback stock photo and the illustration.

**Example:** save your headphones photo as `AJ0013.jpg` in this folder.

Priority per product: `assets/img/products/<ID>.jpg`  →  keyword stock photo  →  built-in illustration.
