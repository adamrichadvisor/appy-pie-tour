Product overview

The whole outbound motion, in one place.

Teams stitch together a data vendor, a verifier, an AI writer, a sending tool, and a spreadsheet. Vantage collapses that stack into a single automated workflow — so a rep goes from a search query to a running, compliant campaign in minutes, not days.

Discover

Search 275M+ contacts across 13 filters. AI enriches each company — services, decision-makers, tech stack, and public contact details.

Verify

Every address runs through MX, SMTP, catch-all, disposable, and role-based checks. Results resolve to Verified, Risky, or Invalid before a single email is sent.

Personalize

The AI writer drafts subject lines, body, CTA, and signature — tuned to industry, role, pain points, and country. Nine template styles, all editable.

Send & warm

Connect Gmail, Microsoft 365, or SMTP/IMAP. Randomized intervals, daily caps, domain warm-up, and bounce handling protect your sender reputation.

Measure

Live campaign progress plus opens, clicks, replies, and bounces. Export any view to CSV or Excel; slice by country, industry, and status.

Stay compliant

One-click unsubscribe, suppression lists, sending-rate limits, and CAN-SPAM / GDPR guardrails are enforced by the platform, not left to the user.

Email Verification

Five checks. Three verdicts. Zero wasted sends.

Every address is validated before it can enter a campaign. The engine runs a layered check and resolves each contact to a single, actionable state — so your sender reputation is never spent on addresses that were never going to land.

  • MX & SMTP validate the domain accepts mail and the mailbox exists.
  • Catch-all & disposable detection flags domains that accept anything or self-destruct.
  • Role-based detection separates info@ / sales@ from real humans.
  • Verified · Risky · Invalid — unlimited verification on business plans.

REST API surface

A clean, versioned API — everything the UI does, you can automate.

Resource-oriented endpoints under /v1, OAuth-scoped tokens, cursor pagination, and idempotency keys on writes. Webhooks push delivery events back to your systems in real time.

  • Idempotent sends prevent duplicate email on retry.
  • Signed webhooks for opens, clicks, replies, bounces, unsubscribes.
  • Scoped tokens — per-workspace, least-privilege RBAC.

Request lifecycle — a send

Campaign start → scheduler enqueues verified-only contacts → send worker claims a job → checks daily cap & mailbox health → waits a randomized 30–90s → sends via provider → records event → webhook fires → analytics rollup updates. Bounces flip the contact to suppressed and halt further sends to that address.

Write path & suppression

leads.verify_status gates campaign membership — only Verified rows are enqueued. Each send appends an immutable row to send_events (sent · delivered · opened · clicked · replied · bounced · failed · unsubscribed). A bounced or unsubscribed event writes to the workspace suppression list, which every future enqueue checks first. Reporting reads are served from a materialized rollup, refreshed on event ingest.

Responsible sending

Compliance is a feature, not a footnote.

Legitimate outreach depends on trust — of recipients, mailbox providers, and regulators. Vantage builds the guardrails into the product so teams stay on the right side of the law and the spam filter.

CAN-SPAM GDPR CASL SPF · DKIM · DMARC

One-click unsubscribe

Every email carries a working opt-out and physical-address footer. Opt-outs write to the suppression list instantly and irreversibly.

Suppression & consent

Global suppression lists, bounce-triggered stops, and consent tracking prevent contacting anyone who shouldn't be contacted.

Rate & volume limits

Configurable per-mailbox daily caps, randomized delays, and warm-up ramps keep sending within provider limits.
