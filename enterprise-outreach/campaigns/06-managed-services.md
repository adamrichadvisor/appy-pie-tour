## Email sequence (4 steps)

**Subject options:** `Who looks after {{company}}'s apps at 2am?` / `{{first_name}}, predictable app support for {{company}}` / `Keeping {{company}}'s apps healthy`

```text
Hi {{first_name}},

Many {{industry}} companies launch apps and portals with an agency, and then the agency moves on. Bugs pile up, OS updates break things, and cloud costs creep upwards.

We take over the running of your web and mobile applications: 24/7 monitoring, bug fixes, OS and security updates, cloud cost optimisation and small enhancements, all under a clear monthly SLA. Our AI-assisted tooling lets us do it at a lower cost than a traditional support team.

Would a free health check of one of {{company}}'s apps be useful?

{{sender_name}}
```
