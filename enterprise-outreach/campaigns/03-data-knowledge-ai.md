**Subject options:** `An AI assistant that knows {{company}}'s documents` / `{{first_name}}, where does your team's knowledge live?` / `Making {{company}}'s data AI-ready`

```text

Most {{industry}} companies have thousands of policies, contracts, manuals and reports spread across SharePoint, drives and email, and staff spend hours searching for answers that already exist.

We build private AI assistants on top of that knowledge. They answer questions with citations to the source document, respect existing access permissions, and run in your own cloud if required. We also build the data pipelines that keep everything current.

Would a 20-minute call to look at one knowledge area at {{company}} be worthwhile?

{{sender_name}}
```

### Step 2 · Day 3 · Sales Pitch

**Subject options:** `What a 4-week knowledge AI pilot includes` / `Re: AI assistant for {{company}}` / `Pilot outline, {{first_name}}`

```text
Hi {{first_name}},

• Pick one document set (e.g. policies, contracts, SOPs or product manuals)
• Build a secure assistant with source citations and role-based access
• Test with 20–50 real users and measure time saved and answer accuracy

It deploys in your cloud tenancy, and your data is never used to train public models.

Shall I send a short proposal for {{company}}?

{{sender_name}}
Enterprise Solutions · Appy Pie
appypie.com · flozic.ai · pixazo.ai
```
