## 1. Executive summary

[Client] is expanding its use of AI while regulatory expectations increase (EU AI Act, GDPR, sector regulators).
We propose a **3-week, fixed-fee AI risk assessment** that inventories AI use, classifies risk, tests the highest-risk systems
and delivers a prioritised roadmap. An optional follow-on implements the controls.

## 2. Your challenges

- No complete inventory of AI systems and 'shadow AI' tools
- Unclear obligations under the EU AI Act and sector rules
- Risk of personal or confidential data leaking to external models
- No consistent testing or monitoring of AI outputs

## 3. Proposed solution

- **AI inventory and discovery:** interviews, tool and network review, vendor AI features.
- **Risk classification** mapped to the EU AI Act risk tiers and ISO/IEC 42001 controls.
- **Security testing** of priority AI use cases: prompt injection, data leakage, harmful or biased outputs.
- **Controls implementation (optional):** AI usage policy, PII redaction gateway, logging, private model hosting, human-oversight workflows.
- **Board-level reporting** in plain language.
