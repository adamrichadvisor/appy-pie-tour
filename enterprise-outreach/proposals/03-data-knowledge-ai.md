## 1. Executive summary

[Client]'s teams spend significant time searching [policies / contracts / SOPs / product documentation] spread across
[SharePoint, Confluence, drives, email]. We propose a **4-week pilot** that delivers a secure AI assistant answering questions
from your own documents **with citations**, respecting existing access permissions and deployed in **your own cloud tenancy**.

## 2. Your challenges

- Knowledge scattered across systems and duplicated versions
- Slow onboarding and repeated questions to experts
- AI pilots blocked by data quality and security concerns
- Risk of staff pasting confidential data into public AI tools

## 3. Proposed solution

- **Data readiness:** connectors, cleaning, de-duplication and metadata for the pilot document set.
- **Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG):** answers grounded in your documents, with source links and 'I don't know' when the evidence is missing.
- **Security:** SSO, role-based access mirroring source permissions, PII redaction and full query logging.
- **Deployment options:** your AWS / Azure / GCP tenancy, with private or managed LLMs. Your data is not used for model training.
- **Channels:** web app, Microsoft Teams or Slack, and API.

## 4. Approach & timeline

| Period | Phase | Activities |

| Week 1 | Data readiness | Select document set, connectors, cleaning, access model |
| Weeks 2–3 | Build | Retrieval pipeline, assistant UI and Teams/Slack integration, evaluation set |
| Week 4 | Pilot | 20–50 users, accuracy and time-saved measurement, results report |
