## 1. Executive summary

We propose that Appy Pie take over the day-to-day running of [Client]'s [apps / platforms] under a **clear monthly SLA**:
monitoring, incident response, bug fixes, OS and security updates, cloud cost optimisation and a monthly enhancement budget.

## 2. Your challenges

- Apps without an owner after the original vendor left
- Crashes, bad reviews and broken features after OS updates
- Rising and unmanaged cloud costs
- Internal IT stretched across too many systems

## 3. Proposed solution

- **24/7 monitoring and alerting** (uptime, crashes, performance, security).
- **Incident management** with defined response and resolution times.
- **Maintenance:** OS / framework / dependency updates and app store compliance.
- **Cloud FinOps:** right-sizing and reserved capacity, with monthly cost reports.
- **Enhancement hours** each month for small features.
- **AI-assisted operations** (log analysis, test automation) to keep costs down.
