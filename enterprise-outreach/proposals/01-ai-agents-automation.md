## 1. Executive summary

[Client] handles a high volume of repetitive work across [support / finance / onboarding / sales operations],
spread over tools such as [Salesforce, Zendesk, SAP, Microsoft 365]. We propose a **6-week, fixed-price pilot** that
deploys AI agents on one high-volume workflow, integrated with your existing systems, with human approval at every
decision that matters. The target outcome is **[30–50]% less manual effort on the chosen workflow**, measured in production.

## 2. Your challenges

- Staff time spent on copy-paste work between systems
- Slow response times and growing backlogs in [support / AP / onboarding]
- Errors from manual data entry and inconsistent processes
- Previous RPA or automation efforts that were brittle or hard to maintain

## 3. Proposed solution

- **Flozic workflow platform:** visual workflows connected to 1,000+ apps, with AI agents (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or a private model) as decision steps.
- **Custom integration layer:** our engineers connect systems that need more than off-the-shelf connectors (legacy databases, internal APIs, SSO).
- **Human-in-the-loop controls:** confidence thresholds, approval queues, full audit log of every agent action.
- **Monitoring dashboard:** volumes, automation rate, accuracy, hours saved and exceptions.
